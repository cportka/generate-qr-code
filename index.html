How it works

  • Private by design. The QR code is generated locally with the MIT-licensed qrcode-generator library, bundled with this page. Your text never leaves your device — there is no server.
  • Higher error correction (Q/H) survives scuffs, logos, and low light at the cost of a denser code. H is the default, matching the command-line script.
  • PNG is best for printing and sharing; SVG scales to any size without blur — ideal for signage and vector art.
